### Post 2: behind the build (image post, a few days later)
Image: `linkedin-process.png`

> Glass, goo, and every fact cited: a few notes from building *Who Held the Glass*.
>
> 1️⃣ **Frosted glass, done lightly.** The chart sits on a milky glass panel so the hand-drawn vines show softly through it, without fighting the data.
>
> 2️⃣ **Goo with a purpose.** The "ooey gooey" feel comes from an SVG filter. When you pick an event, a wine drop slides to its marker and a second drop falls down the timeline and melts into it. It tells you where you are without a word of UI copy.
>
> 3️⃣ **Citations as an interaction.** Every event has numbered source chips. They open a small glass card with the source, and all 67 sources live in a bibliography below the chart. Trust is a design feature.
>
> 4️⃣ **A glass that fills.** A sketched wine glass fills as you move forward through history. It's a progress bar that doesn't look like one.
>
> It's keyboard-friendly and respects reduced-motion settings too, because delight shouldn't lock anyone out.
>
> If your brand has a story worth telling this way, I'd love to hear it.
>
> #uxdesign #interactiondesign #webdevelopment #accessibility #dataviz
