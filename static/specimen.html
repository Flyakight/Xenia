H

Notepaper fading into a screen

Two materials on one surface. Paper is front-lit, warm, grained, soft-edged and never pure white. A screen is back-lit, cool, flat, pixel-gridded, and emits rather than reflects. The trick is that each texture layer carries its own gradient mask, so grain, dot screen, pixel grid and glow each hand over at a different point instead of cross-fading as one block.
