The Hare

Lepus europaeus · giorria

  • a

    Ears laid back along the body. In Irish and Scottish tales, a hare wounded in the night is found by morning as an old woman with the same wound.

    Eyes set high and wide. A hare sees almost all the way around itself without turning its head.

  • c

    The long hind leg. The "boxing" of March hares is usually a female fending off a male who is too keen.

Colophon

Palimpsest, a site and theme for authors and practitioners. Demo author: Wren Ashby. Set in IM Fell English and EB Garamond. The ghost writing under the title is the opening of Pangur Bán, a poem about a scholar and his white cat, written by an Irish monk in the 9th century. The volvelle takes its manner from the paper instruments of 16th-century Italy. The seal reads W and A in Ogham, upward: in the earliest inscriptions Fearn stood for a W sound, and the single cross-stroke is Ailm.
