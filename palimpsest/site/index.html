The Author

ᚁBeith, the birch

I write dark fantasy for readers who suspect the old stories were never really about the past. I grew up between a village library and a hedgerow, and I still can't tell you which taught me more.

My grandmother was a in all but name. She read the cards at her kitchen table and called it paying attention. I read them the same way, as mirrors and never as verdicts, the way the once read a neighbour's trouble.

The Farsee Chronicles began there, with a question I couldn't shake: what if the future were not a promise but a weapon, and the people who could see it were the ones everyone feared?

†hedgewitch
a modern word for an old figure

A solitary practitioner of folk magic, working with plants, weather and the household. The hedge is the boundary between the village and the wild, and she walks it.

‡cunning folk
Britain, roughly 1500 to 1900

Village healers and charmers who were paid to find lost things, cure cattle and break curses. Their neighbours trusted them to undo witchcraft, not to do it.

The Farsee Chronicles

Blythe is Lord of the Tower Guard, first in all his exams and on track to be Lord Protector of the City within the decade. The only thing that can stop his ascent is an uncomfortable truth, unearthed when a hedgewitch reads far more than his fortune in the firelight.

Explore the world of Farsee, where the future is a malleable weapon in the hands of witches and Timetellers, and truth is what you make of it.

For lovers of dark fantasy and justice served sideways.

The Hedgerow

ᚅNion, the ash

A field guide to the plants and creatures in the books, drawn from life and from lore.

  • a

    The young leaves. Country children called them "bread and cheese" and ate them straight from the hedge in spring.

  • b

    The blossom. Never brought indoors, or death follows it in. Its scent shares a chemical with decay, which may be where the warning began.

  • c

    The thorn. A lone hawthorn is a fairy tree. In 1999 a new road in County Clare was bent around one at Latoon rather than cut it down.

❧Tonal studies sit on vellum plates; line drawings can float on the dark. Each one washes in from where the brush began.
