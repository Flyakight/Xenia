Illustrations for the "Who Held the Glass" page (page-who-held-the-glass.php).

Drop these files in this folder. Each can be .png, .webp, .jpg or .svg:

  vine.png       Cream line-art vine on a transparent background.
                 Shown twice on the dark hero (one copy mirrored), grows in and sways.
  mushroom.png   Pencil mushroom. Sits on the cream "cellar book" section.
  morel.png      Grey morel. Also on the cream section.

The mushroom and morel are blended with "multiply", so a white paper
background disappears on its own. No need to cut them out.

Any file that's missing is simply left off the page.
